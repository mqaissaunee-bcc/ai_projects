For faculty: build a planner

The Pathway Pack Builder turns your catalog page and documents into a planner for your program. It takes one sitting, and you review everything before students see it.

  1. Pick your program from the programs in the catalog.
  2. Copy the prompt into Copilot Chat, ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, and attach your catalog page PDF and any other documents.
  3. Paste the reply back. The builder checks it and tells you what to fix.
  4. Review and download a one-file planner to share with students.
