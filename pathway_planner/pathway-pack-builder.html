Pathway Pack Builder
Brookdale Community College

Build a pathway planner for your program.

Describe your program, let an AI assistant read your catalog page and documents, and download a ready-to-post planner your students can use to map their classes, careers, and next steps. You check the result before students see it.

01

Describe your program

Pick your program from the list and the rest fills in. The steps are a starting point; turn any of them on or off.

Steps students will work through

Open the catalog page and use its Download as PDF button. You will attach that PDF in step 2.

02

Ask an AI assistant to draft the pack

Works with Microsoft Copilot Chat, ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Start a new chat, attach your documents, paste the prompt, and send.

Attach: the catalog program PDF (required). Add whatever else you have: a list of which terms each class runs, recommended electives, course syllabi, transfer agreements, certification information. The more you attach, the less you fix by hand.
03

Paste what the AI gave you

Paste the whole reply. The builder finds the pack inside it and checks it.

04

Review and download

The AI drafts; you decide. Check the items below against the catalog, then download the planner and post it where students can reach it.
